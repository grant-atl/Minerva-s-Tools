## Design Context

### Users

Minerva Tools serves designers and front-end developers who need a focused browser utility, want a reliable answer or export quickly, and then want to return to their primary work. The tools should be understandable to first-time visitors without slowing down experienced users.

### Brand Personality

Warm, capable, and quietly premium. The voice is clear, specific, privacy-conscious, and practical—never hypey.

### Aesthetic Direction

Preserve the existing refined, warm interface and the “fast, clear, and quietly premium” product direction. Favor focused interactions, strong defaults, and calm hierarchy over visual or verbal noise.

### Design Principles

1. Make the next useful action immediately apparent.
2. Describe shipped behavior precisely; avoid unsupported speed, cadence, or privacy claims.
3. Keep each utility focused and browser-native, with no account required.
4. Treat privacy as a concrete product behavior and disclose third-party services plainly.
5. Use concise, human language that respects both newcomers and experienced practitioners.
